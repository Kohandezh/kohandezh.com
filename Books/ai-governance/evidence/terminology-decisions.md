# E2 terminology decisions (binding for all new chapters)

Rule: default = the verified INSO Persian term for that concept, in that standard, at that clause. Common/NIST/E1 forms stay as ALIASES
and are shown in parentheses at the FIRST use in each chapter. Never replace a word inside NIST-derived translated text (E1 chapters are not edited).
Never equate terms because they look alike. Source-specific definitions are kept apart and attributed (INSO … بند …).

## Hard content rules (all chapters)
1. Three visible layers: (a) what the standard says (paraphrase + short verbatim definitions, each with clause and page), (b) the compiler's explanation / comparison with NIST, (c) Iranian organizational application. Mark them with the section label. Never blend.
2. Modality: «باید» ONLY where the standard prints a shall-level requirement; «توصیه می‌شود / بهتر است» for recommendations; «می‌تواند / مجاز است» for permissions; plain statements for information. «لازم است / ضروری است / موظف است» are NOT «باید» — record them as printed and say so. Never turn a recommendation into an obligation. Annex 'normative/informative' labels must be reported as printed even when odd.
3. Compiler recommendations are phrased «در لایهٔ اجرایی این کتاب پیشنهاد می‌شود …» and never attributed to INSO/ISO/IEEE/NIST.
4. No claim of certification, accreditation, legal compliance or official endorsement. Conformity with a standard ≠ legal/regulatory compliance (7002 §4.1–4.2, printed p.12). INSO/ISO/IEEE did not review or approve this book.
5. No Iranian law, regulation, authority or mandate may be named or implied except as printed in the supplied standards. Use «ممکن است بسته به صنعت و مقررات حاکم، الزامات دیگری نیز اعمال شود.» Scenarios are anonymous («یک بانک ایرانی»…).
6. Do not describe INSO documents as if they were the international text: always give both identifiers (e.g. «INSO 23733:1403 — معادل یکسان ISO/IEC 23894:2023») and keep the Iranian adoption year apart from the international year.
7. Quotations: only defined terms/definitions, ≤ 45 words each, ≤ 10 per chapter, in blockquote with source line «[INSO 23733:1403، بند ۳.۲، ص ۳]». Everything else paraphrased. Do not reproduce tables or Annex A control statements.
8. Missing standards (ISO/IEC 42005, 42006, 22989, TR 24027, TR 24028, 5338): never describe their content. INSO 23679:1403 is printed in 42001 §2 as «ISO/IEC 22989:2022، همسان»; the file was not supplied, so cite only that identification.
9. Persian typography: ZWNJ in compounds, «»، ارقام فارسی in prose for clause numbers (e.g. بند ۶.۴.۲), Latin acronyms (AIMS, RACI, KPI) kept; English identifiers LTR inside RTL text.
