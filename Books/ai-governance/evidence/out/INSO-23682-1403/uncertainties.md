## B. Digits and numbering
4. ## C. Terminology and normative force
9. Evaluate / direct / monitor vocabulary is inconsistent — PDF 18 (8), 47 (37), 48 (38): 4.3 list = «جهت‌دهی، نظارت، ارزشیابی، گزارش‌دهی» (four items, not three); 4.3 comparison sentence = «ارزیابی، جهت‌دهی، نظارت»; its footnote says INSO 12047 uses «ارزشیابی، هدایت، پایش»; Annex A.1.1 and Figure A.1 use «ارزیابی، جهت‌دهی، پایش». The Persian «نظارت» therefore sometimes stands where INSO 12047 has «پایش» (monitor). The English original was not available, so do not map them one-to-one without checking.
10. ISO/IEC 38500 principles list — NOT present in this document. The only description of the evaluate-direct-monitor model is Annex A.1.1 (one sentence citing INSO 12047:1395) plus Figure A.1 and Figure 3. The INSO text never prints ISO/IEC 38500 as the equivalent of INSO 12047:1395 (neither in clause 2 nor in the bibliography); that equivalence is not verified from this file.
11. Annex A labelled «(الزامی)» — PDF 7 (ز) and 47 (37) — content is descriptive/guidance with recommendation wording; I could not check whether the ISO original labels it informative. Recorded as printed.
12. Modality — «باید» occurs only once in the whole PDF (preface boilerplate, PDF 8; confirmed in the native text layer and on the image). Edge wording: «لازم است» (6.4, PDF 35) and «ضروری است» (A.1.4 PDF 50; also introduction PDF 9 and 6.3 PDF 32) recorded as `info` with a note, not as `shall`; «سزاوار است» (5.5, 6.1, A.1.4) recorded as `should` with a note. Lists introduced by «می‌تواند شامل … باشد» (4.3 directing/oversight/evaluating/reporting; 6.4; 6.7.5) are `may`. Some statements mix info and should; the strongest printed modality was recorded.
13. Typo in the printed text — PDF 11 (1) — clause 1 prints «هنمودهایی» for «رهنمودهایی». The paraphrase uses the intended word.
14. Governing-body naming — Figure 3 (PDF 30) labels the box «نهاد حاکمه», body text uses «نهاد حکمران»; 6.3 once uses «هیئت حکمران». Figure 4 labels («ضوابط مبهم», «رایا تهدیدها», «عدم توضیح‌پذیری ML») differ slightly from the body headings («مشخصات مبهم», «رایاتهدیدها», «عدم توضیح‌پذیری (توصیف‌پذیری)»).
15. 4.1 attribution — PDF 14 (4) — the description of governance and the governing-body/management split is attributed to INSO 13245:1398 cl. 5.3, a standard that the bibliography lists as risk-management guidelines. Plausible (integration clause of a risk standard) but not verified; cite the passage as «INSO 23682, 4.1», not as a governance definition from INSO 13245.
