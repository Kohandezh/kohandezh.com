Stop autonomous execution only when one of these conditions occurs:

1. An operation may destroy production data.
2. A production database migration is irreversible.
3. Required credentials are unavailable.
4. Two plausible target environments exist and choosing incorrectly could affect production.
5. A secret is exposed.
6. A critical security vulnerability is discovered that makes further execution unsafe.
7. A legal or copyright issue prevents safe content processing.
8. A factual claim cannot be verified but is required for publication.
9. The repository is corrupted.
10. Disk, memory, or environment limitations make safe execution impossible.
11. Existing uncommitted changes would be overwritten and ownership cannot be determined.
12. A destructive command is the only available path.

Ordinary design decisions are not hard blockers.

Choose a safe default, document it, and continue.
