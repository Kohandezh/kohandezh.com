Waiting — the MK loading motion

The tile appears, the M sweeps in from the left and the K arms slide in from the right to lock onto the M's right stem, then the green accent slab rises into place from its base. While the page is still loading, the slab slowly empties and refills. When the page is ready, the tile grows slightly as the cover fades.

  • First view of a session: shown at least 940 ms (the end of the entry), then dismissed at load or 1.2 s after the parser finishes, whichever comes first, and after 3 s at most.
  • Later views: dismissed as soon as the parser finishes, and the mark appears already complete.
  • With reduced motion: the logo stays still and only fades.
